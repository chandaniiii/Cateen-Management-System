About FoodExpress

Our Story & Mission

FoodExpress was founded with a simple goal: to make delicious, hygienic, and affordable food accessible to everyone at the tap of a button. Whether you're working at the office, studying at home, or relaxing with friends, our digital ordering platform ensures hot, chef-crafted meals reach your doorstep in minutes.

Customers can easily browse our dynamic food catalog, customize portions, select delivery addresses or store pickup, pay safely with eSewa or Cash on Delivery, and track order progress live from preparation to delivery.

Fresh Ingredients

Locally sourced vegetables, farm-fresh meat, and authentic spices prepared under strict culinary standards.

Transparent Pricing

Fair, honest pricing in NPR without hidden fees, plus free delivery on qualifying orders.

Speedy Delivery

Dedicated riders and smart dispatch keep transit times short so food arrives steaming hot.
